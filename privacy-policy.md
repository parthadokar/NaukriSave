# Privacy Policy — NaukriSave

**Last updated:** April 20, 2026

## What data is collected

NaukriSave captures the following fields when you perform a search on Naukri.com:

- Job keyword / designation
- Experience level
- Location
- Search URL
- Timestamp of the search

## Where data is stored

All data is stored **locally on your device** using `chrome.storage.local`. No data is ever sent to any external server, third party, or developer.

## What data is NOT collected

- Personal information (name, email, phone)
- Login credentials or session tokens
- Browsing history outside of Naukri.com search actions
- Any data from pages other than Naukri.com

## Data sharing

No data is shared with anyone. Ever.

## Data deletion

You can delete individual searches or all saved searches at any time directly from the extension popup. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.

## Permissions used

| Permission | Reason |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Save search queries locally on your device |
| `tabs` | Open Naukri.com in a new tab when you click "Search again" |
| `host_permissions: naukri.com` | Read search fields from Naukri.com pages only |

## Contact

For questions or concerns, open an issue on the GitHub repository.
